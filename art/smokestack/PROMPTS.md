# Old Smokestack: image prompts

Every image the level still needs, so that everything on screen looks hand-drawn like the train.
Send them back numbered; Claude cuts them up and places them (the order and the names below are what
the cutter expects). Scale does not matter: every piece is resized to its place in the game.

If an image comes out with something wrong (a missing piece, two pieces touching, a label), regenerate
just that one.
